Private tours

Expedition days. Reserve nights.

China Adventure Tours designs private journeys across Sichuan, Tibet, Xinjiang, Inner Mongolia’s Kubuqi, and the classic cities — Chengdu soft landings, Chongqing nights, Beijing without the bus. We plan the hard logistics; you keep the days long and the stays high. Lodging runs from Ritz-Carlton Reserve and peer luxury hotels to luxury camping tents where the landscape asks for it.

Sample itineraries

Sample journeys

Sample journeys are starting points — we reshape them around your dates and pace. DM on Instagram to start.

Priority
7–10 days

Sichuan Highland Loop

  • Chengdu soft start → panda morning → highland parks & Tibetan foothill valleys
  • Private panda base timing; tea-house + hotpot night
  • Mountain day with trail choice by fitness; village lunch
  • Optional Jiuzhaigou corridor when season allows

Lodging: City five-star or Temple House–tier in Chengdu; where the loop allows, Ritz-Carlton Reserve Rissai Valley (Jiuzhaigou) or peer highland lodge.

For: Couples and friend pairs who want nature after the city soft landing.

4–6 days

Chengdu Soft Landing

  • Guided panda morning — not the rush slot
  • Kuanzhai alleys without the checklist pace
  • Sichuan opera or face-changing if you want culture
  • Hotpot with heat dialed to the guest

Lodging: Luxury boutique / five-star in Chengdu.

For: First-timers, solo joiners meeting a partner, anyone stacking onto Tibet or Xinjiang.

3–5 days

Chongqing Night City + River Edge

  • Skyline / cable / alley night walk with driver on call
  • Hotpot neighborhood pick
  • Optional Three Gorges day trip or cruise embark add-on
  • Art or museum half-day if rain

Lodging: High-rise five-star with river or skyline view.

For: Urban adventure travelers, food-led trips, add-on to Sichuan.

5–7 days

Beijing Classic — Private, Not Packed

  • Early Mutianyu or Jinshanling walk with picnic
  • Private hutong courtyard cookery
  • Forbidden City timed; Temple of Heaven taiji optional
  • Evening at leisure — not a forced show

Lodging: Peninsula / Opposite House–tier or peer central luxury.

For: First China classic done properly before flying west.

Signature
3–4 nights

Kubuqi Desert Camp Nights

  • Sunset dune walk or off-road approach
  • Stargazing / campfire evening
  • Sunrise on the sand
  • Optional Ordos culture half-day

Lodging: Luxury camping tent / desert camp where the season allows — confirmed with the operator for your dates.

For: Adventure couples, photography trips, short add-on from Beijing.

7–10 days

Tibet — Lhasa + Day Trips, Altitude Care

  • Fly-in Lhasa with buffer day and hydration plan
  • Potala / Jokhang paced; private monastery mornings
  • One longer highland day (Ganden, Yamdrok, or season equivalent)
  • Optional Nyingchi / glamping-style extension

Lodging: St. Regis–tier Lhasa or Songtsam Linka peer; luxury tent where the route allows.

For: Spiritually curious adventurers who still want a proper hotel pillow.

10–14 days

Xinjiang Silk Road Private

  • Urumqi / Turpan / Kashgar corridor
  • Turpan oasis & ruins without group rush
  • Kashgar market timing if dates allow; desert or canyon day
  • Night food streets with a guide who eats there

Lodging: Best available city luxury + quality desert lodge on route where it exists.

For: Long-haul travelers who want west China, not only the east-coast classics.
