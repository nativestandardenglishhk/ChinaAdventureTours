Sample itineraries

Sample journeys

Each sample is a starting carriage — we reshape the route around your dates and pace. DM on Instagram to begin.

Priority
7–10 days

Sichuan Highland Loop

  • Leave Chengdu after a panda morning; climb toward highland parks and Tibetan foothill valleys
  • Tea-house light and a hotpot night before the road turns steep
  • A mountain day shaped to your legs; lunch in a village that still keeps time
  • Optional Jiuzhaigou corridor when the season opens the gates

Nights: A quiet city room in Chengdu; on the loop, a good bed after the pass — highland lodge or valley stay when the road allows.

For: Couples and friend pairs who want nature after the first city days.

4–6 days

Chengdu First Days

  • A guided panda morning — not the rush slot
  • Kuanzhai alleys at a walking pace, not a checklist
  • Sichuan opera or face-changing if the evening calls for it
  • Hotpot with the heat dialed to whoever is at the table

Nights: A quiet room in the city — somewhere you can sleep well before the road opens west.

For: First-timers, solo joiners meeting a partner, anyone stacking onto Tibet or Xinjiang.

3–5 days

Chongqing Night City + River Edge

  • Skyline, cable, alley — a night walk with the driver on call
  • Hotpot in a neighborhood that still smells of chili oil
  • Optional Three Gorges day, or a cruise embark if the river is calling
  • An art or museum half-day when the rain settles in

Nights: High above the river or the neon — a room with a view of the city waking after dark.

For: Urban travelers, food-led trips, an add-on after Sichuan.

5–7 days

Beijing Classic — Private, Not Packed

  • Early wall at Mutianyu or Jinshanling, picnic in the quiet
  • A hutong courtyard kitchen, cooking with whoever lives there
  • Forbidden City on timed tickets; Temple of Heaven taiji if you want the morning slow
  • Evenings left open — no forced show, no bus schedule

Nights: A central room with calm after the courtyards — somewhere to rest before flying west.

For: First China classics done properly before the train of the journey turns toward the west.

Signature
3–4 nights

Kubuqi Desert Camp Nights

  • Sunset on the dunes, or an off-road approach while the light goes copper
  • Stars and a campfire when the wind drops
  • Sunrise on the sand before the heat finds you
  • Optional Ordos culture half-day on the way out

Nights: Camp under desert stars — luxury tents where the season allows, confirmed for your dates.

For: Adventure couples, photography trips, a short add-on from Beijing.

7–10 days

Tibet — Lhasa + Day Trips, Altitude Care

  • Fly into Lhasa with a buffer day and a hydration plan
  • Potala and Jokhang at a human pace; private monastery mornings
  • One longer highland day — Ganden, Yamdrok, or whatever the season opens
  • Optional Nyingchi extension when you want the forest edge of the plateau

Nights: A quiet room in Lhasa with altitude in mind; canvas or lodge further out when the route allows.

For: Travelers drawn to the high plateau who still want rest after the thin air.

10–14 days

Xinjiang Silk Road Private

  • The corridor from Urumqi through Turpan toward Kashgar
  • Oasis and ruins without a group clock ticking behind you
  • Kashgar market when dates allow; a desert or canyon day between stations
  • Night food streets with a guide who eats there

Nights: The best city bed available, then a desert lodge where the road still holds one.

For: Long-haul travelers who want west China, not only the east-coast classics.
